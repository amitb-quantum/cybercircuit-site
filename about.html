About Cyber-Circuit

Cyber-Circuit tests its claims against evidence.

Cyber-Circuit is focused on one hard cloud-security problem: determining what the available evidence establishes about the effect of a proposed permission withdrawal on a real observed operation.

The system is built around captured authorization evidence, bounded evaluation, explicit refusal conditions, and deterministic re-derivation of recorded evaluations.

Principles

What the system refuses to compromise.

Evidence before assertion

Claims stay inside what the evidence supports.

Unknown is valid

Missing evidence produces UNKNOWN, not fabricated certainty.

Deterministic by design

Recorded evaluations can be re-derived from captured inputs.

Bounded claims

Cyber-Circuit does not claim universal IAM completeness or global permission safety.

The limits described on this page are the ones the software actually has — we'd rather publish them than have you discover them in your account.

Origin
